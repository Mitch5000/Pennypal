PennyPal — two aesthetic directions

Both kill the generic navy+neon-emerald look and both riff on the brand name — PennyPal → a copper/penny accent. They differ on the one big fork: warm light vs. warm dark. Same layout, same data — only the identity changes.

Palette: warm charcoal (green-brown undertone, not blue-navy), warm off-white ink, copper primary, muted sage-green for income, warm rust for spending. All gradients flattened. Type: Manrope or keep Inter. Same structural overhaul I did on ClientFlow, but a green/copper identity so the two apps never look alike.

Both directions also remove the 👋 emoji, the purple→indigo avatar gradient, the emerald→teal logo gradient, and every decorative gradient — replaced with flat tinted chips and a real hand-drawn mark, like I did for ClientFlow.
